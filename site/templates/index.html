The arithmetic between a telecom network and a billing system.

mediation normalises, deduplicates, correlates and reconciles usage records from mobile and fixed-line networks — and proves it lost none. Octets, seconds and events out; pricing stays with your rater.

Every record reaches exactly one outcome.

carried_in + ingested == billable + suppressed + duplicate + rejected + held

How the pass works

Scope

In

  • Validated identities — IMSI, MSISDN, IMEI, SUPI (incl. wireline), SUCI, PLMN
  • Deduplication, sequence gaps, partial reassembly, correlation
  • Wire codecs — BER, TBCD/BCD, AddressString, PLMN, ULI
  • Dial plans and destination classes
  • Validation rules V01–V21, release gate, conservation law
  • Roaming reconciliation, EU/German regulatory arithmetic, fraud evidence

Out

  • Collection — transports are I/O
  • Rating — no currency or tariff type exists here
  • Online charging — a live conversation with a CHF
  • Orchestration — no scheduler, queue or store
